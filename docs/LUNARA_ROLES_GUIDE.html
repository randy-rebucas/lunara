How Lunara works — one guide per role

Four apps.
One laundry order, start to finish.

Every Lunara order passes through four different people, each looking at a different app. This guide walks through what each role sees and does — and how their four apps line up into a single order, end to end.

Customer · books & pays Shop Partner · washes & readies Rider · picks up & delivers Admin · runs the network

customer-web · customer-mobile

Customer

Books a pickup, pays, and tracks laundry from doorstep to doorstep.

Booking now runs on flat-priced bag sizes, not a weight guess — pick a bag, actual weight is confirmed at the shop.
01

Book a pickup

Pick an address, a nearby shop, a service, and a bag size — then add optional extras like express return or ironing.

02

Pay at checkout

GCash, Maya, card, wallet balance, or cash on pickup — whichever the shop and address support.

03

Track in real time

Watch status move from shop-assigned → picked up → processing → out for delivery, with live rider and shop updates.

04

Rate the order

Leave a star rating and comment straight from the tracking screen once laundry is delivered.

partner-web

Shop Partner

Runs the laundry shop that receives, washes, and hands orders back for delivery.

Auto-accept incoming orders in Settings skips the manual tap — the shop accepts the moment dispatch assigns it.
01

Accept the order

New orders land in the Incoming queue. Accept by hand, or let auto-accept clear them instantly if enabled.

02

Receive & verify

When the rider drops off laundry, verify the actual weight and items against the order.

03

Process the load

Move it through wash, dry, and fold/iron steps on the shop's processing queue.

04

Request delivery

Mark ready-for-delivery, then run Settings, Inventory, Staff, Services and Revenue from the same portal.

rider-mobile

Rider

Ferries laundry between customer and shop, both directions.

Shops can set a default rider. Online, they're assigned instantly; offline, the job broadcasts to every other online rider nearby.
01

Go online

Start a shift from Home to begin receiving pickup and delivery offers.

02

Take the job

Get assigned directly as a shop's default rider, or accept an offer broadcast to nearby online riders.

03

Verify & collect

Confirm the customer, weigh the bag, capture photo proof, then drop the laundry at the shop.

04

Deliver & remit

Bring finished laundry back to the customer, collect cash if needed, and track earnings in the wallet.

admin-web

Admin

Watches over every shop, rider, and order across the network.

Automation Settings can auto-assign pickup and delivery riders platform-wide, on top of each shop's own default-rider setup.
01

Watch dispatch

Monitor the live queue of orders waiting on a shop or rider, and step in when one stalls.

02

Manage the network

Onboard partner shops and riders, tune branch pricing, and set service coverage areas.

03

Handle exceptions

Resolve support tickets, refunds, and lost-item reports that need a human decision.

04

Track the business

Review revenue, settlements, and promotions across every shop from one dashboard.
